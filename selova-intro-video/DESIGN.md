# Selova Intro Video Design

## Style Prompt

Fast, premium AI-product launch energy for a Japanese iOS learning app. The screen recording is the hero: a tall iPhone UI floats in a deep graphite product-stage with precise data lines, editorial kinetic type, and short high-contrast Japanese copy. The mood should feel like a sharp ChatGPT-era product reveal, but warmer and more study-focused than generic neon tech.

## Colors

- Background: `#080A0F`
- Surface: `#111720`
- Foreground: `#F6F1E8`
- Muted foreground: `#A8B0AE`
- Primary accent: `#F2055C`
- Growth accent: `#19D27F`
- Warm signal: `#F3B94E`

## Typography

- Primary: `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `SF Pro Display`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
- Numbers and labels: `SF Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace

## Motion

- Snappy 0.35-0.7s entrances with vertical lift, scale, and opacity.
- Scene copy changes on beat every 4-6 seconds.
- Background lines drift slowly with finite GSAP repeats.
- Phone footage stays readable; overlays frame the UI instead of covering it.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make the palette only cyan or purple-blue.
- Do not use gradient text.
- Do not cover important app UI with large captions.
- Do not use stock abstract AI visuals instead of the real app recording.
- Do not add long explanatory text.
